Refund Policy

Effective: 29 September 2026 · WiFi Voucher

This Refund Policy explains how WiFi Voucher handles paid subscriptions.

Trial

The free trial is provided so you can test the service before paying. No refund is due for a trial because no subscription fee is charged for the trial.

Paid subscriptions

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable once a paid subscription period has started, except where a refund is required by applicable law or where we approve a refund for a duplicate or clearly erroneous charge.

Service problems

If a material service problem prevents you from using the core service, contact us promptly with the affected account and dates. We may review the circumstances and, where appropriate, provide a credit or other remedy.

How to request a review

Email support email not configured with your account email, payment reference and a description of the issue. Do not send router passwords.

Changes

We may update this policy when pricing, billing methods or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be posted here.
