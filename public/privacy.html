Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 September 2026 · WiFi Voucher

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information WiFi Voucher collects, why it is used, who it may be shared with, and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as name and email address.
  • Router information you choose to provide, including label, network host, API port and API username. Router passwords are encrypted at rest and are not displayed back to the browser.
  • Voucher, profile and subscription records needed to provide the service.
  • Support or payment information you voluntarily send us.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

Why we use it

We use information to create and secure accounts, connect to authorized MikroTik routers, generate and synchronize vouchers, provide subscriptions and billing support, prevent abuse, maintain security, and respond to support requests.

Data minimisation

We aim to collect only information necessary for these purposes. Optional fields are identified where practical. We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information that is unrelated to operating this service.

Third parties

The service uses Supabase for authentication and database infrastructure, Render or an equivalent hosting provider for application hosting, and Google only when you choose Google sign-in. We do not use advertising networks or analytics trackers in this build.

International processing

Infrastructure providers may process or store information outside Uganda. Where applicable, we will use appropriate safeguards and/or obtain consent as required by applicable law.

Retention

We retain account and operational records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet contractual or legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact support email not configured with your request.

Security

We use access controls, database row-level security, encrypted router credentials, HTTPS in production, rate limits and other safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Uganda data protection

Where Uganda law applies, our privacy practices are intended to be read together with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021. This page is a product privacy notice, not legal advice.
